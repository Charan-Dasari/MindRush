HOW TO PLAY MINDRUSH

1

Position Yourself & Controls

Sit 2–3 feet in front of your screen. You can control the paddle using your mouse, touchpad, arrow keys, or webcam gesture tracking.

2

Collect High-Value Badges

Catch Droplets (+10), Sunbursts (+20), Spiral Shells (+25), and Bronze Stars (+50). Grab Heart-Diamonds for 1UP extra lives!

3

Avoid Deadly Hazards & Traps

Steer clear of spiky naval mine hazard bombs (-1 life), purple trap shurikens (-20 pts), and cross hazards.

4

Conquer Hard to Unlock Insane

Scoring 1,000+ points on Hard mode permanently unlocks the high-octane Insane difficulty tier!
