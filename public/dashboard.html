MISSION GUIDE

HOW TO PLAY MINDRUSH

1

Open Palm: Movement

Skyfall & Space Invader

Show your open palm and move your hand left or right to control the paddle or spaceship.

2

V Sign: Pause / Resume

All Modes

Raise your index and middle fingers in a V shape to pause or resume the game.

⏱️ Hold the gesture steadily for a brief moment to activate.
3

Closed Fist: Shoot

Edu Space Invader

Make a fist to shoot continuously in Educational Space Invader. Release your fist to stop shooting.

🎯 Applies to Educational Space Invader only — Skyfall uses paddle catching.
4
OPTIONAL

Keyboard Controls Available

Optional

Prefer keyboard controls? Switch to Keyboard Controls from the Control Method setting in the pause menu.

Skyfall ← → or A D to move
Educational Space Invader ← → or A D to move • Space to shoot
