HOW TO PLAY MINDRUSH

Control the game with simple hand waves

1

Position Your Webcam

Sit 2 to 3 feet in front of your camera in a well-lit room with your palms visible.

2

Read the Equation

Check the math problem (e.g. 9 × 8 = ?). Multiple candidate numbers will float down.

3

Catch the Correct Answer

Wave your palm to swat or touch the correct answer orb (72) before it falls.

4

Build Score Streaks

Solve consecutive equations correctly to earn multipliers and unlock trophies.
